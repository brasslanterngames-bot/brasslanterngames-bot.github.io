Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to us at brasslanterngames@gmail.com. Please mention your device and the game version (Settings → Credits & licences).

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device, automatically after every turn — even in the middle of a volley. There is no cloud save, so uninstalling the app or erasing data removes your progress.

I bought something on a new phone and don't have it.

Open Support the game from the main menu and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

An ad didn't load, did I lose my reward?

No. If no ad is available the game tells you, nothing is used up, and you can try again a moment later.

How do I erase my data?

Settings → Erase save data. It asks twice, and it cannot be undone. Purchases can be restored afterwards.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your device language. To choose another one, open Settings → Language.

The game feels too hard or too easy.

Eclipse levels raise the difficulty after your first win. If you're stuck, try Ivo, the most forgiving hero, and look for relics that add caroms.
