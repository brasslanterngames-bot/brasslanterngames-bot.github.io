Caromancer

Every bounce makes your spell stronger.

A turn-based roguelite you play with one thumb. Drag to aim, let go, and watch your orbs ricochet through a tide of rune-carved golems.

How a turn works

  1. Aim

    Drag anywhere on the board. The aim line shows where your first bounces land.

  2. Bank it

    Every wall or ceiling bounce adds a carom: +50% damage for that orb.

  3. Shatter

    Golems step down after each volley. Clear the floor before they reach your lantern.

Ten places, ten bosses

Each run crosses three acts drawn from ten places that open up as you beat their guardians. Every place has its own golems and its own boss — a Geode Queen whose shell grows back, a Clockmaker who rewinds damage, a Hollow Star that swallows your aim line.

Builds that get out of hand

Draft from 14 orbs and 42 relics between fights. Splitting Twins, chain-lightning Volts, exploding Novas, Comets that launch pre-charged — stack them until one volley does thousands of damage.

Fair by design

  • No energy, no timers, no loot boxes.
  • Ads only play when you choose one: to rekindle a run or double your Starlight.
  • Everything that changes gameplay is earned by playing.
  • Works fully offline. No account.

Plenty to come back for

Six heroes, ten Eclipse difficulty levels, a Daily Rift, a Weekly Challenge, Boss Rush, six Trials, Endless mode, 39 feats, hero mastery goals, shareable seed codes and 13 orb styles. In English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch and 日本語.
