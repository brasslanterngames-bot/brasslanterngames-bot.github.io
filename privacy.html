Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Caromancer has no accounts, and Brass Lantern Games does not collect your personal data. Your progress is saved only on your device. On phones, two services built into the app may process limited data: Google AdMob, for ads you choose to watch, and RevenueCat, for purchases.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings and the run in progress are saved in the app's storage on your device. They are never sent to us. You can erase them at any time in Settings → Erase save data, or by uninstalling the app.

Optional ads (Google AdMob)

On iPhone and Android, an ad only plays when you choose one — to rekindle a run, double your Starlight or restock the merchant for free. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. While the app is open, the ad SDK runs in the background so an ad is ready when you choose one. If you own the Supporter Pack, it is not started at all.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form. On iPhone, iOS asks whether the app may track you; if you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (App Store, Google Play, RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Purchases are verified and restored through RevenueCat, using a random anonymous ID. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and basic technical information such as the app version, operating system and country. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

No accounts or sign-in. No analytics or crash reporting of our own. We don't sell or share data. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Web version

The browser version saves progress in your browser's local storage. It shows no ads and makes no real purchases.

Children

Caromancer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google or RevenueCat, use their privacy tools, or write to us and we will help you reach them. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have further rights; contact us and we will respond.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
